Features

Everything you need to run your store

A complete toolkit derived from KasbPro's functional requirements (FR-01 to FR-07).

Secure User Authentication

Role-based login for Business Owners, Sales Staff, and Admins so sensitive financial data stays in the right hands.

FR-01

Inventory Management

Add, update, and organize products by price, quantity, and category — replacing manual stock counts with a live digital ledger.

FR-02

Digital Billing (POS)

Search products and generate professional digital invoices instantly — speeding up checkout and giving customers proper receipts.

FR-03

Automated Stock Updates

Every successful bill automatically deducts inventory, so the stock count you see is always the stock you actually have.

FR-04

AI Sales Analytics

Visual trends and demand forecasts on your historical sales — so you know exactly which products to restock first.

FR-05

Customer CRM

Store customer phone numbers and purchase history for loyalty tracking and future marketing campaigns.

FR-06

System Maintenance

Admins can run backups and manage user account permissions to keep the system stable and secure from data loss.

FR-07

Web-Based Accessibility

Use KasbPro on any device with a browser — no installs, no setup. Just sign in and run your store from anywhere.

How It Works

Get up and running in four steps

A simple, web-based workflow designed for small-business owners with no technical expertise.

1

Create your account

Register as an Owner for full control, an Admin to help run daily operations, or Staff to handle checkout.

2

Stock your inventory

Admins add products, set prices and categories. Stock counts are kept live and accurate.

3

Bill customers digitally

Use the POS to generate invoices in seconds. Inventory updates automatically with each sale.

4

Review AI insights

Owners and Admins see sales trends, low-stock alerts and customer activity in one dashboard.

Owner

Full management access

  • Add, edit, delete products, suppliers & customers
  • Manage user accounts & Admin/Staff permissions
  • Run backups & system maintenance
  • View all sales history, invoices & AI insights

Admin

Operational access

  • Add & edit products, suppliers, customers
  • View sales history, invoices & AI insights
  • Cannot delete records or manage user accounts

Sales Staff

Billing access

  • Generate invoices through the POS
  • Add new customers at checkout
  • Cannot access inventory, suppliers, or analytics
