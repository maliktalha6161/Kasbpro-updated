About Us

KasbPro: Your Partner in Retail Success

Founded with the mission of simplifying retail management, KasbPro is dedicated to helping small business owners optimize their operations with smart, easy-to-use, role-based solutions — built as a Final Year Project at Government Jinnah Islamia Graduate College, Sialkot.

Our Objectives

Centralize Business Operations

One web-based platform where small businesses manage customers, inventory, and billing efficiently.

Simplify Client Management

Store, view and manage complete customer information and transaction history in an organized way.

Improve Inventory Control

Real-time stock tracking with low-stock alerts to avoid shortages and over-stocking.

Digitize Billing Processes

Automate invoice generation and maintain accurate sales records with zero manual effort.

Support Decision-Making

AI-assisted insights — sales summaries and stock recommendations — that help owners make informed calls.

Web-Based Accessibility

Use KasbPro from any device with a browser — no installs, no fuss, no IT team needed.
